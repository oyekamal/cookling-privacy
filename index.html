Cookling Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-17

Cookling is a cooking-lesson app. It does not collect, send or share any personal data.

What stays on your phone. The app saves your progress (such as the name you give your chef friend, the dishes you have cooked, the allergens you chose to avoid, and your settings) in the app's private storage on your device. This information never leaves your device.

What we do not do. Cookling has no account or sign-in. It has no ads. It has no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools. It does not use the internet, so it cannot send data anywhere. It does not ask for access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Third parties. Cookling includes no third-party services that receive your data.

Deleting your data. Clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app, and all saved progress is removed.

Children. Cookling collects no data from anyone, including children. Cooking involves knives, heat and hot oil, so younger children should cook with an adult.

Changes. If this ever changes, for example if a feature needs the internet, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
